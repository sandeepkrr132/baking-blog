Rustic Sourdough Bread

Rustic Sourdough Bread is a homemade delight, featuring a chewy crust and a tangy, flavorful interior that's perfect for any meal.

Prep Time
30 min
Cook Time
45 min
Servings
8
Difficulty
Intermediate

Ingredients

  • 500g bread flour
  • 350g warm water
  • 100g active sourdough starter
  • 10g salt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil (for bowl)

Instructions

1

In a large bowl, mix flour and water until no dry bits remain. Let rest (autolyse).

⏱️ Autolyse: 30:00
2

Add sourdough starter and salt. Mix and fold until fully incorporated.

⏱️ Mix & fold: 10:00
3

Perform stretch and folds every 30 minutes for the first 2 hours.

⏱️ Stretch & folds: 120:00
4

Cover and let the dough rise at room temperature until doubled.

⏱️ Bulk fermentation: 240:00
5

Turn dough onto lightly floured surface. Shape into a round boule.

6

Place seam-side up in a floured banneton or bowl. Cover and refrigerate overnight.

⏱️ Cold proof (or overnight): 480:00
7

Preheat oven to 500°F (260°C) with Dutch oven inside for at least 45 minutes.

⏱️ Preheat Dutch oven: 45:00
8

Carefully place dough in hot Dutch oven. Score the top with a razor blade.

9

Bake covered for 30 minutes, then remove lid and bake until deep golden brown.

⏱️ Uncovered bake: 15:00
10

Transfer to wire rack and let cool completely before slicing.

⏱️ Cool completely: 60:00
